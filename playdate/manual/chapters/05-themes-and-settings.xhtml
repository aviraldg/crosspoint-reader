Themes and settings

Open Settings from the home screen. Settings are grouped by category.

Two themes are designed for Playdate. Choose one under Display, then UI Theme.

  • Crossplay shows your current book and its cover beside a list of places to go.
  • Playful shows your current book on a tall card beside animated tiles.

In these two themes, the button hints at the bottom of the screen hide after a few seconds. Press a button or turn the crank to show them again.
