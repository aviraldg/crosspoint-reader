Reading

Open a book from the home screen or the file browser. The app remembers your page in every book.

The first time you open a large book, the app prepares its chapters. This can take a moment. Later visits are faster.

Press A while reading to open the reader menu. From there you can choose Select Chapter, change Text Settings, and Go Home.

Text settings change the font, size, spacing, and margins. The app lays out the book again after a change.
