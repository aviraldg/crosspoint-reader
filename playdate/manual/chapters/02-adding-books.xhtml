Adding books

Books live in the Books folder of the app's Data folder on your Playdate.

  1. Connect your Playdate to a computer with a USB cable.
  2. On the Playdate, open Settings, then System, then Reboot to Data Disk.
  3. On the computer, open the PLAYDATE disk and go to Data.
  4. Open the folder that ends with {{bundle_id}}. A sideloaded copy uses a folder name that starts with user..
  5. Copy your .epub, .txt, or .xtc files into its Books folder.
  6. Eject the disk. The Playdate restarts.

If the Data folder is missing, start {{app_name}} once. The app creates the folder on first launch.
