{{app_name}} is an e-book reader for Playdate. It reads EPUB, TXT, and XTC books that you copy to the device.

This manual is a normal book in your library. Press A while reading and choose Select Chapter to jump to a chapter. The last chapters list the licences for the software and fonts that {{app_name}} includes.

When an app update changes the manual, the app replaces this book and it opens at the start again. If you delete the manual, the app does not add it back. Your own books are never changed.
