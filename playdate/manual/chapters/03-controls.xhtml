Controls

D-pad: move through lists and turn pages.

A: open the selected item. In a book, A opens the reader menu.

B: go back.

Crank: undock the crank to scroll lists and turn pages. Each step is 36 degrees, so one full turn moves ten steps. Turn it clockwise for the next page and counter-clockwise for the previous page.

Menu button: opens the Playdate system menu. It has shortcuts for Reader Menu, Go Home, and Dark Mode.
